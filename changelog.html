What's new in Quickler.

Dated record of what has shipped: new compliance workflows, dashboard improvements, customer launches and platform changes. Most recent first.

2026-05-26 — Public positioning rewrite

Site, llms.txt and llms-full.txt now lead with the WhatsApp field compliance positioning instead of the older "workflow systems" framing. New public FAQ page with 15 question-and-answer pairs covering EICR, CP12 gas safety, van checks, structural reports, pricing and data ownership. Sitemap pruned of legacy project pages.

2026-05-20 — Lab–prod cron disabled, full security audit complete

Following a full audit, the automatic lab-to-prod promotion cron is paused. Patches are now landing in controlled waves with explicit verification at each step.

2026-04-24 — Cost optimisation plan: hot-path Claude calls to be switched to Groq Llama 3.3 70B

Plan agreed to migrate hot-path AI calls to Groq for an estimated 95% cost reduction without behavioural change to the engineer-facing flow.

2026-04-21 — RI Cruden goes live on Scale tier

First UK construction firm running daily van safety checks through Quickler on WhatsApp. Drivers walk round the vehicle, open WhatsApp, answer the DVSA-aligned check. Defect photos go straight to the fleet manager; vehicles with red status do not leave the yard.

2026-04-15 — Guided narrative profile shipped

New workflow profile for professional assessment reports (used by Ethos Safety). Sits between the open report and verdict slot-fill profiles, supporting professional-grade assessment narratives with structured evidence capture.

2026-04-13 — Public site reorganised, llms.txt and site.json published

Marketing site reorganised into clear lanes. First publication of llms.txt, llms-full.txt and site.json so AI search engines can read the site without scraping HTML.

2026-03-16 — Quickler Ltd incorporated

Quickler Ltd registered in Scotland, company number SC882439. ICO registration C1910464 follows shortly after.
