What is Quickler?

Your team already does the inspections. Quickler does the paperwork.

Most field teams already capture the job on their phone — a few WhatsApp messages, a photo, a voice note. The problem is what happens next: someone has to write it up into a proper report, certificate or compliance record. That is the bit Quickler removes.

A worker answers Quickler's questions in WhatsApp on site. Quickler turns those replies and photos into a clean, timestamped digital record automatically, flags any faults, and produces a PDF whenever a client or regulator needs one. No app to install, no per-user licence, no end-of-day desk work.

Who it is for: field teams, inspectors, surveyors, maintenance and compliance teams — any firm with a repeatable check or report that still gets written up by hand afterwards.

Field compliance reporting for UK and global trades

Quickler is a WhatsApp inspection app for field teams anywhere. Engineers use WhatsApp — which is already on their phones — to complete compliance checks on site. Quickler collects the responses and produces a clean, structured digital record automatically, which exports to a PDF report whenever one is needed. There is no separate app to install and no per-user licence fee. Because it runs entirely over WhatsApp, Quickler works in any country — it is used in the UK, Europe and the United States.

It covers EICR electrical inspection reports, CP12 gas safety certificates, daily van safety checks, construction site inspection reports, and any other field compliance workflow a firm repeats regularly. Firms switching from iAuditor or SafetyCulture typically go live within a week.
