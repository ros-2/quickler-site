# Quickler photo shot-list

The new homepage has photo slots (the placeholder blocks marked
"Photo: ...") ready for real images. Replace them as you get shots.
Priority order below. Real photos beat stock every time for a
compliance product, so even rough phone shots of real jobs win.

## Style rules (so they look consistent and premium)
- Real, not stock. A genuine van / fuse board / muddy site reads as
  credible. Stock smiling-office-people reads as cheap.
- Natural light. Avoid flash. Shoot near a window or outdoors.
- Landscape orientation, high resolution (phone is fine on a modern
  phone — just don't crop in hard).
- Leave some empty space in the frame; the layout overlaps text/edges.
- One subject per shot. Uncluttered.

## Priority 1 — the hero (single most valuable shot)
**An engineer on site, phone in hand, mid-job.**
- A real trade person (electrician/gas/site) holding a phone, clearly
  working — fuse board, boiler, van, or site behind them.
- This is the "capture happens on the phone" moment. It carries the
  whole pitch.

## Priority 2 — "the problem" section
**Paperwork / a clipboard / forms at a desk.**
- The pain you remove: a stack of job sheets, a clipboard, a laptop
  with a half-typed report at a kitchen table in the evening.
- Slightly tired/cluttered is good here — it's the "before".

## Priority 3 — "the shift" section
**A phone showing a real WhatsApp job thread.**
- A genuine phone screen with a Quickler WhatsApp conversation
  (questions + a photo reply). Hold the phone in a real setting.
- Replaces the current cheap-looking mock panel. Far more convincing.

## Priority 4 — texture shots for "what it handles"
Trade-specific reality. One each, used small:
- A fuse board / consumer unit (EICR)
- A gas meter or boiler (CP12)
- A work van, front 3/4 angle (van checks)
- A construction/site scene (site inspections)

## Priority 5 — proof (over time, as you get customers)
- Real customer sites, vans, teams (with permission).
- A finished PDF report on a screen or printed in a hand (the "after").
- These become your strongest social proof — a logo wall or named
  testimonial with a face converts better than any copy.

## Where each goes
| Section | Slot label on site | Shot |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | "engineer on site, phone in hand" | Priority 1 |
| The problem | "paperwork / clipboard at a desk" | Priority 2 |
| The shift | "phone showing a real WhatsApp job" | Priority 3 |
| What it handles | (texture, optional) | Priority 4 |
| Who it's for / proof | (future) | Priority 5 |

When you have any of these, drop the file in `assets/photos/` and tell
me which section — swapping a placeholder for a real image is a
two-minute change.
