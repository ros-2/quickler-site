Quickler in Canada

WhatsApp field compliance software in Canada.

Canadian field firms already coordinate crews over WhatsApp. Quickler turns that same channel into inspections, checklists and certificates. A worker on a site in Toronto, Calgary or Halifax answers questions by text, voice note or photo, and every answer becomes a clean, structured digital record on a manager dashboard, with red, amber and green fault status. When a formal document is needed, a PDF exports straight from that record. No app to install, no per-user fee, no hardware to ship across the provinces.

The output

A structured digital record, not just a piece of paper.

Most field tools leave a manager with a stack of PDFs to read one by one. Quickler leads with something more useful: a live, machine-readable record of every job, sortable and searchable on one dashboard.

  • Every inspection lands on the dashboard with red, amber and green fault status
  • Managers see what failed, where, and who recorded it without opening a file
  • Photos and voice notes attach to the exact question they answer
  • A PDF exports from the record whenever a client, insurer or regulator wants a document

For a Canadian firm running checks across multiple sites and time zones, that shift from a pile of documents to one structured record is the practical difference.

No app, no friction

Crews use the WhatsApp account already on their phone.

Field work in Canada often means subcontractors, seasonal crews and bilingual teams who change from job to job. Asking each of them to download, log in to and learn a new app is where adoption stalls.

  • The field worker opens WhatsApp, which they already use every day
  • There is nothing to install, no account to create, no password to reset on site
  • It works the same on any Canadian carrier and on any phone
  • Because there is no per-user fee, adding a casual crew member costs nothing

The barrier to getting a worker recording proper compliance data drops to one message.

Common questions

Running Quickler in Canada.

Do my crews need to install anything?

No. They answer in WhatsApp on the phone they already use. There is nothing to download and no per-user fee.

What do I actually get back?

A structured digital record on a manager dashboard with red, amber and green fault status. A PDF exports from it whenever you need a formal document.

Can it run my own Canadian forms?

Yes. Quickler is job-agnostic. Upload any inspection or checklist form and Quickler builds it into a WhatsApp workflow.

Where does my data sit?

On EU servers in Germany, with a GDPR-aligned design. It is a privacy-respecting base independent of any single app store or carrier.

What does it cost?

Pricing is quoted in GBP, with EUR and USD available on request. There is no per-user charge, so cost does not climb with crew size.
