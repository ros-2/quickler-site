For US field teams

Field inspection software your crew runs over WhatsApp

US contractors, facilities teams and fleet operators waste hours on clipboard forms and apps nobody opens. Quickler runs your inspection, safety walk or vehicle check as a WhatsApp conversation. The worker answers by text, voice note or photo on the phone in their pocket, and you get a clean digital record back. No app to install, no seat licences to buy.

The friction

Apps your crew never opens

Most field inspection software asks each technician to download an app, create an account and learn a new interface. On a busy job that is exactly the step that gets skipped. Checks end up on paper, in a notes app or in someone's head, and the office spends the evening chasing photos.

  • No install means nothing to push to a new hire on day one.
  • No per-user fee means a seasonal or sub-contracted crew costs nothing extra to bring on.
  • WhatsApp is already on the phone, so adoption is immediate.

The output

A structured record, not a photo dump

Every completed check lands on a manager dashboard as a live, machine-readable record with a red, amber or green status against each item. You can filter by site, crew or fault, see exactly which photos back which answers, and act on the failures the same day.

When a document is needed for a client, an auditor or your own files, a clean PDF exports straight from that record. The structured data comes first; the PDF is there when paperwork demands it.

Site safety

Self-inspection walks over text

Upload the safety self-inspection checklist your business already uses, including formats modelled on OSHA self-inspection guidance, and Quickler turns it into a guided WhatsApp walk. The supervisor works through it on site, snaps a photo of any hazard, and the record is flagged the moment it is logged.

Quickler is not an official OSHA product and does not certify compliance. It captures, structures and timestamps the inspection your own process requires, so the evidence exists when you need it.

Fleet and equipment

Vehicle checks drivers actually finish

Driver vehicle inspection reports, pre-trip and post-trip checks, equipment and lone-worker sign-offs all run the same way. Upload the sheet, including DOT-style fleet check formats, and the driver completes it over WhatsApp before they pull out of the yard.

  • Defects are flagged red the instant they are reported.
  • Voice notes capture detail a driver would never type.
  • The whole fleet's status sits on one dashboard.

Questions from US teams

Field inspection software, answered

Does my crew need an app?

No. Workers answer over WhatsApp with text, voice notes or photos. There is nothing to install, no login to manage and no per-user seat fee.

Can it run OSHA-style safety walks?

Yes. Upload your own self-inspection checklist, including OSHA-modelled formats, and it becomes a WhatsApp workflow. Quickler is not an official OSHA product and does not certify compliance.

What about DOT-style fleet checks?

Upload a driver vehicle inspection or fleet sheet, including DOT-style pre-trip and post-trip formats, and drivers complete it over WhatsApp. Each check becomes a structured record with fault status and PDF export.

Where is the data stored?

On EU servers in Germany, handled in line with GDPR, regardless of where your crews work.

What language and pricing?

English today with more languages on the roadmap. Pricing is in GBP, with EUR and USD on request. Ask on the contact page.
