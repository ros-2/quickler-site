Inspection and checklist software for any trade

Most inspection apps are built for one job. Quickler is built for the shape of the job. The problem with trade-specific tools

One tool per job does not scale

A firm rarely does just one kind of check. The same team that completes an electrical report on Monday is checking a van, a fire door or a piece of lifting equipment by Friday. Buy a separate app for each and you end up paying for several systems, training people on several interfaces and chasing records across several dashboards.

Quickler removes the per-trade boundary. The questions change from one form to the next, but the way a field worker answers and the way a manager reviews stay identical.

The Quickler approach

Workflow first, trade second

Quickler is a workflow engine. It does not assume your job is an EICR or a CP12. You give it a form and it builds a guided conversation around that form, with the right gates, the right photo prompts and the right pass or fail logic.

  • Upload the form you already use
  • Quickler maps its fields and logic into a WhatsApp flow
  • Field workers answer by text, voice note or photo
  • Every job lands as a structured record, ready to export

What the field worker sees

WhatsApp on the phone they already carry

There is nothing to download and no login to remember. A worker opens WhatsApp, answers each question as it arrives and sends a photo or a voice note where it helps. The workflow keeps them on track and prompts for anything missed.

That low barrier matters most where it is hardest to roll out software: vans, ladders, plant rooms and remote sites where people do not want another app.

What the manager gets

A live, structured record, not a pile of PDFs

Every answer flows into a clean, machine-readable record on a manager dashboard, with red, amber and green fault status so problems are obvious at a glance. You can search, filter and act on the data while the job is fresh.

When you need a formal document, export a PDF straight from that record. The record is the source of truth; the PDF is a convenience. Data is hosted on EU servers in Germany and is GDPR-aligned. Read more about how Quickler works.

Common questions

Inspection and checklist software, answered

Is it tied to one trade?

No. Quickler is job-agnostic. It is a workflow engine, not an EICR or gas tool. Upload any inspection or checklist form and the same engine runs it.

What is the output?

A clean, structured digital record, live on a dashboard with red, amber and green fault status. A PDF can be exported whenever a formal document is needed.

Does it need an app?

No. Field workers answer over WhatsApp by text, voice note or photo. No install and no per-user fee.

Does it work abroad?

It works anywhere WhatsApp does. Built around UK compliance, made to work across markets including the UK and beyond.

How do I move my form across?

Send the form you already use. Quickler turns its questions and pass or fail logic into a guided WhatsApp workflow. Start from the demo or the guides.
