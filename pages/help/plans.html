Payment

We collect by Direct Debit. You authorise once; we draw monthly from your bank account. No card numbers stored. You're protected by the Direct Debit Guarantee — refunds are immediate if anything goes wrong.

Upgrading

Hit a limit and the dashboard prompts you to upgrade. You pay the pro-rata difference for the rest of the current cycle; the new price applies from the next one.

Downgrading

Remove engineers until you fit within the lower plan, then accept the downgrade prompt. New price takes effect next cycle.

Cancelling

Email hello@quickler.co. Your subscription ends at the next billing date. Export your data first — see Data & privacy.

Invoices

Every payment generates an invoice emailed to your primary admin. Need a historical one resent? Email support.

Still stuck? Email hello@quickler.co — we reply within one business day.
