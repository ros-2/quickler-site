Field inspection and compliance software for Australia and New Zealand

From WHS site safety walks to pre-start vehicle checks, Quickler turns your existing forms into WhatsApp workflows your crew answers on the phone they already carry. Every check lands as a clean digital record, with a PDF export whenever you need to hand something over.

Site safety

WHS checks that crews actually complete

Work health and safety duties under the model WHS framework rely on records that are done on the day, not reconstructed at the end of the week. The problem is rarely the form. It is getting a busy crew to fill it in.

Quickler removes that friction. A site supervisor answers the safety walk inside WhatsApp, by text, voice note or photo, and the record is built as they go:

  • Toolbox talks, SWMS sign-on and daily site inductions
  • Plant and equipment pre-use checks
  • Hazard and near-miss capture with a photo attached
  • Lone and remote worker check-ins

Anything flagged shows up red on the manager dashboard, so a hazard does not sit unseen in a glovebox.

Fleet and plant

Pre-start vehicle and plant inspections

Pre-start checks are the backbone of fleet and plant safety across mining, civil, transport and trades. A driver should not need a clipboard, a paper book or a separate app to log one.

With Quickler the operator runs the pre-start from WhatsApp before the shift starts:

  • Light vehicle, truck and trailer pre-start checks
  • Earthmoving and plant daily inspections
  • Defect reporting with photos of the fault
  • Odometer, hours and fuel capture in the same flow

A flagged defect is visible to the manager the moment it is logged, so an unroadworthy vehicle is grounded before it leaves the yard rather than after.
