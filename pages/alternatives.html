Quickler vs iAuditor (SafetyCulture)

iAuditor is the dominant inspection app worldwide. Strong template library, offline-first capture, mature mobile app. Per-user pricing.

Pick Quickler if: your engineers won't install another app, you want one per-firm price, you want voice notes captured and auto-transcribed.

Pick iAuditor if: you need offline-first capture in areas with no signal, you want the marketplace of pre-built templates outside UK trades, you have a global rather than UK-only operation.

Honest take: iAuditor is genuinely good. The single biggest reason small UK trades firms switch off it is engineers not opening the app on site. If that's your problem, Quickler removes it because WhatsApp is already open.

Quickler vs SimPro

SimPro is a full field service management platform: scheduling, dispatch, quoting, invoicing, asset management, plus inspection forms.

Pick Quickler if: you only need the compliance-paperwork half — capturing checks on site and producing structured PDF reports. You already have a way to schedule and invoice.

Pick SimPro if: you need one platform to run the whole business, you have 30+ engineers, you have budget for a 4-to-12 week onboarding.

Honest take: SimPro and Quickler barely overlap. Quickler is for firms whose problem is "the report write-up at the desk takes too long". SimPro is for firms whose problem is "we run our whole business out of three spreadsheets and a WhatsApp group".

Quickler vs Joblogic

Joblogic is a UK-focused field service management suite — scheduling, asset tracking, PPM, mobile app, full back office.

Pick Quickler if: the FSM you have is fine; you just want compliance reports done faster without engineers learning another app.

Pick Joblogic if: you need a whole-business UK FSM and you have the operations capacity to onboard one.

Honest take: Joblogic suits a firm of 15+ engineers with a dedicated operations manager. Below that, the per-user pricing and onboarding effort outweigh the benefit and Quickler does the compliance-paperwork job alone at a tenth of the all-in cost.

Quickler vs Fergus

Fergus is a tradesperson-focused job management system from New Zealand, popular with UK plumbers and electricians. Quoting, invoicing, scheduling, mobile app.

Pick Quickler if: you already have your job and invoicing system sorted; compliance reports are the bottleneck.

Pick Fergus if: you need a whole-business job manager and inspection forms are a secondary concern.

Honest take: Fergus's mobile app gets adopted because tradespeople need it to see their next job. The same engineers often refuse to open it for a 12-question safety check at the start of the day. Quickler picks up that specific gap.

Quickler vs FleetCheck

FleetCheck is a UK fleet management platform with a driver app for daily walkrounds, service scheduling, MOT and tax tracking.

Pick Quickler if: the daily DVSA walkround is your main pain point and your drivers won't install the FleetCheck app. Your other fleet admin is fine in a spreadsheet.

Pick FleetCheck if: you need full fleet management — service intervals, MOT diaries, fuel records, driver licence checks — not just the walkround.

Honest take: For a 5-to-30 van firm whose drivers ignore the FleetCheck app, Quickler over WhatsApp produces the audit-ready walkround record with zero adoption fight. For a 50+ vehicle operation that needs the full fleet picture, FleetCheck is the right pick.

Quickler vs iCertifi

iCertifi is a UK certificate-generation app with a deep catalogue of electrical and gas certificates. App-based, per-user pricing.

Pick Quickler if: engineer adoption of yet another app is the blocker; you want the certificate flow to run inside WhatsApp instead.

Pick iCertifi if: you need the breadth of certificate templates iCertifi has built up over years and your engineers are comfortable with the app.

Honest take: iCertifi has the widest catalogue of UK certificates in the market. Quickler is narrower today but trivial for engineers to adopt. If your problem is breadth-of-certificate, iCertifi. If your problem is adoption, Quickler.
