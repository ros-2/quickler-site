Quickler in Ireland

WhatsApp field compliance software in Ireland.

Field firms in Ireland already run on WhatsApp. Engineers, inspectors and site teams send photos and voice notes from the van without thinking about it. Quickler puts the inspection itself inside that same chat: a worker answers questions over WhatsApp, and every completed job becomes a clean, structured digital record on a manager's dashboard, with red, amber and green fault status. A PDF exports from that record the moment a document is needed. No app to install, no per-user fee.

The output

A structured record first, a PDF when you ask for one.

Most field tools treat the finished job as a flat document and stop there. Quickler treats it as data. When an Irish engineer finishes a check, the answers, photos and condition ratings land on a dashboard your office can actually work with.

  • A live, machine-readable record per job, not a static file
  • Red, amber and green fault status so a manager sees risk at a glance
  • Photos and voice notes attached to the exact question they answer
  • Search and filter across jobs instead of opening PDFs one at a time
  • A clean PDF exported on demand when a client or regulator needs the paper

The PDF is a convenience, not the product. The value is the record underneath it, which the office can sort, chase and report on long after the van has left site.

No app, no friction

Built on the phone your team already uses.

Ask an Irish field worker to install and log in to yet another app and adoption stalls before the first job. Quickler removes that step entirely by running over WhatsApp, which is already on the phone.

  • Nothing to download, no account setup for the worker in the field
  • Answers by text, voice note or photo, whichever is fastest on site
  • Works on any handset and any Irish mobile network
  • No per-user fee, so a growing crew does not grow the bill

The workflow guides the worker question by question, so a new hire produces the same complete record as a twenty-year veteran, with no training day.

Common questions

Running Quickler in Ireland.

Do my engineers need to install anything?

No. They use the WhatsApp account already on their phone. There is nothing to download and no per-user fee for the field team.

Can it run my own inspection forms?

Yes. Quickler is job-agnostic. Upload any inspection or checklist form and it becomes a guided WhatsApp workflow, whatever the trade.

Where does the data live?

On EU servers in Germany, GDPR-aligned. As Ireland is in the EU, your records stay within the European Union.

What do I get at the end of a job?

A structured digital record on the dashboard with fault status, photos and answers, plus a clean PDF on export. See how it works anywhere on the global field compliance page, or the Ireland detail on field compliance software for Ireland. To start, get in touch.
