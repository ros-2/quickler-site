No app to install

No-app site inspections for Canadian field teams.

Picture a crew arriving on a site outside Calgary at first light. The driver needs a vehicle pre-use check, the foreman needs a site safety walk, and the operator needs to log the condition of the lift before it runs. Today that is three paper forms on a clipboard, or three logins to a tablet app nobody wants to carry. With Quickler it is one WhatsApp chat on the phone each of them already has. They answer by text, voice note or photo, and every check lands as a structured digital record a manager can read in real time.

A day on site

Three checks, one chat, nothing to download.

The reason paper and tablet apps slow a field crew down is friction at the edges. Paper has to be carried, photographed and re-typed back at the office. Tablet apps need a device per worker, a login, an install and a software update before anyone can start. Quickler removes both by running the inspection inside the messaging app already on the phone.

  • The driver runs the vehicle walkround before pulling out, answering each item in the chat
  • The foreman runs the site safety walk on arrival, snapping photos of any hazard straight into the thread
  • The operator logs the equipment pre-use check, flagging a worn sling with a voice note
  • A new starter needs no setup; once they have your number they can complete their first check
  • No tablets to buy, charge, track or replace when one is dropped on a job

The crew never leaves WhatsApp, and the office never waits for paper to come back from the field.

What the manager sees

A live record, not a stack of PDFs to chase.

Each completed check becomes a clean, structured digital record on a manager dashboard. It is machine-readable and searchable, so a supervisor can see every crew and every site without opening a single attachment.

  • Red, amber and green status surfaces faults the moment a worker flags them
  • The worn sling or the cracked windscreen shows up against the asset, not buried in a photo roll
  • Records are searchable across drivers, vehicles, equipment and sites over time
  • When a client, insurer or auditor wants a document, you export a PDF from the record in one click

The PDF is there when you need it, but it is the convenient export, not the system of record. The structured data underneath is what lets a manager spot a recurring fault before it becomes an incident.

Built to fit your forms

Bring the checks you already run.

Vehicle and fleet

Pre-use and daily walkrounds

Upload your daily vehicle or trailer inspection and Quickler builds it into a WhatsApp workflow. Drivers run it before they move; defects are logged against the unit with a photo, ready for the next inspection cycle.

Equipment and plant

Pre-use condition checks

Lifts, slings, ladders, generators and powered tools all have a pre-use check worth recording. Operators flag a fault by voice note or photo, and it carries forward so the same item is followed up next time.

Site safety

Arrival and toolbox walks

A site safety walkround, hazard board or toolbox check runs the same way. The foreman answers in the chat, hazards are logged with location and photo, and the record is on the dashboard before the walk is finished.

Common questions

Running no-app inspections in Canada.

Is there really nothing to install?

Correct. The crew uses WhatsApp, which is already on the phone. No app store download, no login to provision, and no per-user fee as the team grows.

Can I run vehicle, equipment and site checks?

Yes. Quickler is job-agnostic. Upload any inspection or checklist form and it becomes its own WhatsApp workflow, so one crew can run all three from the same chat.

Do I get a document for my records?

Every check is a live structured record on the dashboard with red, amber and green status. When you need a document, you export a PDF from that record in one click.

Will it work across remote Canadian sites?

Quickler runs anywhere WhatsApp does, so a remote crew works exactly as a city one. Read more on the field compliance that works anywhere page, or see the workflows on offer.

Where is the data held?

On EU servers in Germany (Hetzner), with a GDPR-aligned design. Workflows run in English today, with more languages on the roadmap.
