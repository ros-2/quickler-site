Field compliance in India

WhatsApp field compliance software in India

Field-services firms across India already run on WhatsApp. Quickler turns that same chat thread into a structured inspection tool: a field worker answers questions by text, voice note or photo, and every job lands as a clean digital record on a manager's dashboard. No app to install, no per-user fee.

The starting point

Built on the channel India already trusts

India runs one of the largest WhatsApp user bases in the world, and field teams lean on it for everything from job allocation to sending site photos. The problem is that those photos, voice notes and messages scatter across a hundred chats and never become a usable record.

Quickler closes that gap. Instead of asking a technician, electrician or facilities engineer to learn a new app, it meets them where they already work:

  • Questions arrive in WhatsApp, one at a time.
  • Answers come back as text, a voice note or a photo.
  • The worker uses the phone in their pocket, with no install and no login to remember.

For firms managing distributed crews across cities and states, that removes the single biggest barrier to adoption: getting people to use the tool at all.

The output

A structured record, not a buried chat

The point of Quickler is what comes out the other side. Each completed job becomes a clean, structured digital record on a live manager dashboard, with red, amber and green fault status so a supervisor can see at a glance what passed and what needs action.

Because the record is machine-readable, you can search it, filter it and report on it across your whole operation rather than scrolling through threads.

  • Live status across every site and every crew.
  • Faults flagged and tracked, not lost in a group chat.
  • A PDF export available whenever a printed or signed document is needed.

The PDF is the convenience, not the product. The record is the asset.

Any job shape

One tool across India's field-services landscape

India's field-services market is broad and fast-moving: facilities management for offices and malls, electrical and HVAC maintenance, fire-safety inspections, fleet and van checks, equipment servicing, construction site walks and retail audits. Most of these still run on paper or on a separate app for each task.

Quickler is job-agnostic. Upload the form you already use, and Quickler builds it into a WhatsApp workflow. One firm can run several different inspection types through the same channel, each producing its own structured record.

It is not an electrical tool or a fire tool. It is whatever your form says it is.

Reach and data

Works anywhere WhatsApp does

Quickler is rooted in UK compliance work and built to run anywhere WhatsApp does, including across India's mix of metro and remote sites. Patchy connectivity is less of a problem when the interface is a chat thread rather than a heavy app.

Data is hosted on EU servers in Germany and the platform is GDPR-aligned. Workflows run in English today, with more languages on the roadmap. Pricing is in GBP, with EUR and USD available on request.

Quickler is built to work anywhere and is used across markets including the UK and beyond.
