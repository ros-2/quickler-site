How It Works

Four steps from site to finished document.

1. Capture

Capture on site

Staff send text, voice notes, photos, and measurements to a workflow number while they are on site, not after they get back to the desk.

2. Extract

Sort the facts into the right structure

quickler reads the messages, recognises the workflow you configured, and slots the content into the correct report, record, or template structure.

3. Review

The engineer stays in charge

The responsible person checks the draft, edits anything they want, and signs off. Nothing ships without a human eye.

4. Output

Finished report or admin pack

Produce the PDF, the document, or the next workflow state. Store the evidence alongside it so the record stands up later.

Supported workflows

Compliance workflows Quickler runs over WhatsApp.

Electrical

EICR — Electrical Installation Condition Report

EICR capture on WhatsApp with photo evidence. C1, C2 and FI codes flagged on the dashboard the moment they are entered. Formatted EICR PDF for the qualifying electrician to sign off. Full guide →

Gas Safe

CP12 — Landlord Gas Safety Record

Appliance-by-appliance checks on WhatsApp with photo of each appliance plate. Formatted CP12 PDF produced for the Gas Safe registered engineer's signature. Stored against the property automatically. Full guide →

Fleet

Van safety checks — DVSA daily walkround

Drivers receive the walkround on WhatsApp at the start of every shift. Defect photos go straight to the fleet manager. Red, amber and green status per vehicle live on the dashboard. Full template →

Structural

Site inspection reports — structural

Capture site notes, photos, sketches and measurements on WhatsApp during the visit. Structured draft report ready by the time the engineer is back at the desk. Accountable engineer reviews and signs. Full template →
