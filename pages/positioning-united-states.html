WhatsApp Field Compliance Software in the United States

Field-service firms across the United States already run their crews on the phones in their pockets. Quickler turns that habit into a compliance system: inspections, checklists and certificates run over WhatsApp, and every visit becomes a clean, structured digital record a manager can read in real time.

How it works

The job runs in a chat the crew already knows

A technician in the field opens WhatsApp, the same way they message dispatch, and Quickler walks them through the inspection one question at a time. They answer however suits the moment:

  • Type a quick reply when they are standing in a quiet mechanical room.
  • Send a voice note when their hands are busy or it is loud on site.
  • Snap a photo when the evidence is easier to show than to describe.

There is no app to download, no login to provision for each new hire, and no per-user fee. A crew that grows for a busy season does not change the bill. For firms running 1099 contractors and seasonal labor across several states, that matters: the tool is whatever phone the worker already has.

The output

A structured digital record, not a stack of paper

The point of the conversation is the record it produces. As the crew answers, Quickler builds a live, machine-readable record on a manager dashboard, with red, amber and green fault status against every line so the office sees a failing item the moment it is logged, not at the end of the week.

When a signed document is needed for a client file, a permit pack or an insurer, a PDF exports straight from that record. The document is a convenience on top of the data, never the only copy. Nothing lives on a clipboard in a truck, and nothing has to be re-keyed back at the office.

The US field-services landscape

One platform across a wide field of inspection work

Job-agnostic

Built around your form, not ours

US field work spans HVAC service calls, electrical and panel checks, plumbing and backflow, fire and life-safety, elevator and facilities rounds, fleet and DOT-style vehicle checks, and construction punch lists. Quickler does not ship a fixed inspection. You upload the form you already use and it becomes a WhatsApp workflow.

Frameworks

Fits the checklists you answer to

Whether your crews work to OSHA-style safety routines, internal QA sheets, or a client's bespoke audit, the structure lives in your uploaded form. Quickler is not an official product of any regulator; it simply turns the checklist you are accountable for into a guided conversation.

Built to travel

Rooted in UK compliance, made for anywhere

Quickler grew out of UK inspection regimes such as EICR and vehicle van checks, then was built to work wherever WhatsApp does. It is used across markets including the UK and beyond, so a US firm gets a platform shaped by hard compliance, not a thin form-builder.

Common questions

US field firms ask us

Do my crews need to install anything?

No. If a worker has WhatsApp, they are ready. The workflow arrives as a normal chat, so onboarding a new hire is sending them a message, not a software rollout. See the global field compliance overview for the full picture.

Is the result just a PDF?

The result is a structured digital record on a dashboard with live fault status. A PDF is one export from that record when you need a document to send. The data comes first, the document second.

Can it handle our own inspection form?

Yes. Quickler is job-agnostic. Upload any inspection or checklist and it is built into a workflow. Browse the workflow types to see the range it already covers.

Where does our data live and how do we pay?

Records sit on EU servers in Germany and the platform is GDPR-aligned. Workflows run in English today, with more languages on the roadmap. Pricing is in GBP, with USD available on request.
