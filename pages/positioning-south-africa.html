Quickler in South Africa

WhatsApp field compliance software for South African firms.

Field firms in South Africa run inspections, checklists and certificates over WhatsApp, with no app to install and no per-user fee. A field worker answers on the phone they already carry, and the answers become a clean structured digital record on a manager dashboard. From spaza shop site checks to electrical compliance work and fleet walkrounds, the record is live, machine-readable and graded red, amber or green, with a PDF export available the moment a document is needed.

The output that matters

A structured digital record, not a stack of paper certificates.

Most field compliance in South Africa still ends in a paper pad, a photo dumped in a phone gallery, or a form typed up back at the office hours later. Quickler changes the output. As a field worker answers over WhatsApp, the work becomes a live record on a dashboard the office can see straight away.

  • Every answer, photo and voice note lands against the right line item in real time
  • Each item is graded red, amber or green, so a manager sees faults at a glance
  • The record is machine-readable, so it can be searched, filtered and reported on
  • A PDF certificate exports from that same record when a client or regulator needs a document

The dashboard is the product; the PDF is a convenience on top of it. That is the difference between a record you can manage a business from and a folder of scanned forms.

South Africa's field landscape

Built for how South African field teams actually work.

South Africa runs a large, spread-out field-services economy: electrical and solar installers, plumbing and HVAC crews, security and facilities firms, mining contractors, fleet operators, construction site teams and lone workers covering wide areas. WhatsApp is already the default way these teams communicate.

  • Field workers use the WhatsApp account on their own phone, with nothing new to issue
  • No rugged tablets or kiosks to ship to remote sites, mines or farms
  • Text, voice note or photo all work, so a worker is never blocked by a form
  • One dashboard pulls every job back to a head office in Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban or anywhere else

Quickler is rooted in UK compliance work, but it is built to work anywhere WhatsApp does, and South African field firms fit that shape closely.
