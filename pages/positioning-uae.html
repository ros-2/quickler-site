Quickler in the UAE

WhatsApp field compliance software for the United Arab Emirates.

Across the UAE, field teams inspect towers, malls, plant rooms, fleets and sites every day, then lose hours turning paper and photos into something a manager can act on. Quickler removes that gap. Your fielders answer the inspection on WhatsApp, the account already on their phone, and every check lands as a structured digital record on a manager dashboard. There is nothing to install and no per-user fee. When a formal document is needed, a PDF exports straight from the record.

How it works on site

The fielder uses WhatsApp; the manager gets a live record.

WhatsApp is the default way people in the UAE communicate, on site and off it. Quickler runs the inspection inside that same conversation, so a worker on a rooftop in Dubai or a warehouse in Sharjah needs no new login, no download and no training.

  • The fielder answers each question by text, voice note or photo
  • Quickler asks for what is missing and confirms as it goes
  • Every answer is captured as structured data, not a free-text mess
  • The result appears on a dashboard with red, amber and green fault status
  • A PDF exports from that record when a client or authority needs a document

The point is the structured digital record. It is live and machine-readable, so a head office can see results across an estate without waiting for paper to travel back from the field.

Any job, not one industry

Upload your own form and Quickler builds the workflow.

Quickler is not a single-trade tool. It is rooted in UK compliance work — EICR, gas safety, van checks — but the engine is job-agnostic. The UAE's field-services landscape spans high-rise facilities management, district cooling and HVAC, fire and life safety, fleet and logistics, construction, oil and gas support, retail and hospitality maintenance. Quickler runs the lot from the same place.

  • Upload your existing inspection, checklist or certificate form
  • Quickler turns it into a guided WhatsApp workflow automatically
  • Facilities rounds, fire checks, plant inspections and fleet walkrounds all run the same way
  • Condition ratings, defect logging, photos and on-site acknowledgement carry across every job

If your team inspects something and writes it down, Quickler can run it, whatever the trade and wherever in the Emirates the work happens.
